You're probably collecting data. But are you actually using it?

Most small businesses have Google Analytics installed and... that's it. The data sits there, unloved, while decisions get made on gut feeling. One client was spending money on Google Ads, content, and networking, but had no idea which one was actually generating business. Every marketing decision was a guess.

## What you get

Clarity on what's working and what isn't. Simple dashboards that tell you what you need to know, not everything that's technically measurable. And crucially, proper attribution so you can see exactly where each lead comes from and what it cost.

- **Proper tracking setup** - Rip out the broken stuff, measure what matters
- **Conversion tracking** - See which channels actually generate leads
- **Custom dashboards** - Your numbers at a glance, not buried in reports
- **Cost-per-lead visibility** - Know what you're paying for every enquiry
- **Privacy-friendly options** - GDPR-compliant alternatives to Google Analytics

## How it works

1. I audit your current setup (there's usually something broken, or missing entirely)
2. We agree what actually matters to track for your business
3. I fix the foundations and set up proper conversion tracking
4. You get a dashboard you'll actually look at
5. I show you how to read the data and act on it
