Email is still one of the best ways to reach customers, it just needs to be done properly. Most problems aren't about what you're saying, but whether your messages actually arrive. Get the infrastructure right first, and the rest follows.

## What you get

Email systems that actually deliver, both technically and commercially. From infrastructure fixes to nurture sequences, I handle the full stack.

- **Deliverability audit** - Fix the technical foundations so your emails land in inboxes
- **Warm email** - Welcome and nurture sequences that keep you in touch without being annoying
- **Cold outreach** - Clean lists, compliant, properly warmed, targeted

If you need more specialised work - CRM integration, segmentation, reputation recovery, custom automation - that's part of what we do too.

## How it works

1. Audit the infrastructure and map the customer journey emails
2. Design the sequences, set up the automation, connect it to your CRM
3. Show you how to keep it running and when to adjust

## When this might be for you

- Your emails go to spam (or you suspect they might)
- You've got leads but no follow-up, or you're sending the same messages by hand
- You want to scale outreach without it feeling like spam
