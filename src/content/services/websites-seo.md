Your website should bring you business, not just exist. And once it's built, people need to find it.

- **Fast, modern build** - Near-perfect Lighthouse scores, clean technical foundations, no plugin sprawl
- **Built to be found** - Local SEO, technical SEO, and AI-readable structure so your content gets surfaced
- **Low-maintenance** - Designed for businesses that don't need to change copy every week

## How it works

1. Talk through the business, audit what's there, agree the plan
2. Build the site with SEO and performance foundations, no bloat
3. Hand over a site you own, with docs and a straightforward update process

## When this might be for you

- Your current site embarrasses you (or doesn't reflect the quality of your work)
- You're invisible on Google for what you actually do
