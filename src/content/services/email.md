Email is still one of the best ways to reach customers, it just has to be done properly. Most problems aren't about what you're saying, but whether your messages actually arrive. Get the infrastructure right first, and the rest follows.

## What you get

- **Deliverability audit** - Fix the technical foundations (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so your emails land in inboxes
- **Sender reputation** - Diagnose what's dragging you into spam and put it right
- **Nurture sequences** - Welcome and follow-up emails that keep you in touch without being annoying

If you need more specialised work - segmentation, reputation recovery, custom automation - that's part of what we do too.

## How it works

1. Audit the infrastructure and map the emails your customers should be getting
2. Fix deliverability, then design and automate the sequences
3. Show you how to keep it running and when to adjust

## When this might be for you

- Your emails go to spam (or you suspect they might)
- You've got leads but no follow-up
- You're sending the same messages by hand and want them to run themselves
