## The short version

I'm a tech consultant based in Surrey, working with small businesses across the UK. My background is in data engineering, platform management, and technical project delivery. I've built data warehouses in BigQuery, managed platforms serving 75,000+ users, and run technical projects for companies like Docker. Now I bring that same rigour to smaller teams, without the complexity or the price tag.

## Why I do this

Small businesses deserve better than overpriced agencies, cookie-cutter solutions, and "experts" who disappear after the invoice is paid. I've seen too many business owners burned by tech projects that promised the world and delivered confusion.

I work differently. I start by understanding what you're actually trying to achieve: mapping your tools, your workflows, and where time and money are being lost. Then I tell you honestly what's worth doing, and what isn't. No upselling, no jargon, no solutions looking for problems.

## How I work

I use modern tools (including AI) to work faster and smarter. That means quicker turnaround and better value for you. But the tools are just tools. What matters is understanding your business and delivering something that actually helps.

Where I'm different from most consultants: I work across data, web, and marketing automation. That means I can spot connections between your analytics, your website, your CRM, and your outreach, and build systems where they reinforce each other instead of sitting in silos.

You work with me directly. Not a rotating team, not a junior who's "learning on the job". When you have a question, you get an answer from someone who knows your project inside out.
