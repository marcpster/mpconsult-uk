## The short version

I'm a tech consultant based in Surrey, working with small businesses across the UK. My background is in data engineering, platform management, and technical project delivery. Now I bring that same rigour to smaller teams, without the complexity or the price tag.

## My approach

I start by understanding what you're actually trying to achieve: mapping your tools, your workflows, and where time and money are being lost. Then I tell you honestly what's worth doing, and what isn't. No upselling, no jargon, no solutions looking for problems.

I use modern tools (including AI) to work faster and smarter. That means quicker turnaround and better value for you. But the tools are just tools. What matters is understanding your business and delivering something that actually helps.

I work across data, web, and marketing automation, so I can spot where your analytics, website, CRM, and outreach should be reinforcing each other instead of sitting in silos.
