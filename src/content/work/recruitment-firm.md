## The problem

SearcHive's team were spending 35 hours a month on manual outreach admin. Six different tools, none of them talking to each other. Contacts scattered across spreadsheets and LinkedIn. Email deliverability was suffering because the basics (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC) weren't set up properly, meaning messages were landing in spam instead of inboxes. Outreach coverage sat at around 60%.

## What I did

Mapped every step of their outreach workflow to find where time and money were being lost. Designed a streamlined stack: Clay for data enrichment, SmartLead for cold email, HubSpot for warm pipeline. Replaced six disconnected tools with three that actually work together. Fixed their email infrastructure from the ground up: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, domain warming, and proper sending configuration. Built webhook integrations so data flows automatically between systems instead of being manually copied.

## The result

The numbers tell the story: 27 hours of monthly admin eliminated, email outreach coverage up from 60% to 85%, and an estimated 13 additional placements per year from the improved pipeline. Total annual value created: approximately £140,000. The team now spend their time placing candidates, not wrangling spreadsheets.
