## The problem

A design studio had great work but a website that didn't show it. Their existing site scored 65/100 on performance: slow to load, clunky on mobile, and impossible to update without calling a developer. All new business came from word-of-mouth and Instagram. They were completely invisible on Google, missing out on people actively searching for designers in their area. They'd been quoted six months and a significant budget by a web agency.

## What I did

Built a fast, modern site using a lightweight framework designed for speed, not a bloated WordPress theme. Showcased their portfolio with full-width imagery that loads instantly. Set up local SEO: optimised for the search terms people actually use when looking for interior designers, added proper schema markup, and configured local business listings. Made the site easy for them to update themselves. Adding new projects takes minutes, not a developer call.

## The result

Launched in four weeks. Performance score jumped from 65 to 99 out of 100. They're now visible on Google for local searches, getting qualified leads from people actively looking for designers in their area. A channel that simply didn't exist before. And they can update the site themselves, keeping their portfolio current without ongoing costs.
